Milk Deliveries

Ereteti Dairy Sacco collects milk from farmers twice a day along our main collection routes.

Delivery Process

  1. Farmers bring their milk to the collection centre.
  2. The milk is weighed and recorded.
  3. Accepted milk is collected for processing.
  4. Farmers are paid according to the milk delivered.

Collection Routes

  • Ngong route
  • Karen route
  • Kiserian route
